Privacy policy

FitPlan · last updated 3 November 2026

FitPlan is a student project of Unicorn University (BPMI course) by Jakub Kalina and Ran Regev. It helps you plan workouts and log your training.

What we store

  • Your Google account ID, so your data belongs only to you.
  • The workout templates and workouts you create in the app.
  • A log of actions in the app (for example "routine created"), linked to your account ID.

Your name, email address and profile picture come from Google sign-in. They are shown in the app and kept only in your browser's sign-in token; we do not store them in our database.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We do not read anything from your Google account except your basic profile.

Where the data is

The app runs on Vercel and Render, and the data is stored in MongoDB Atlas (Frankfurt, EU). All connections are encrypted (HTTPS / TLS).

Deleting your data

You can delete your workout templates in the app at any time. To delete all your data, open an issue in the project repository and we will remove it.
